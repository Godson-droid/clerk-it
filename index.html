The story

How the tournament works

Clerk It began as an arbitrage idea. A census of HSKSwap found exactly one deep pool, so there was no second leg to arbitrage against. The tournament is the honest answer to that fact: prove a strategy in public, on this pool, before a single real unit moves.

01

Four clerks, one pool

Clerks A to D watch the same WHSK/USDT pool with different mean reversion bands, from Tight at 75 bps to Fat Tail at 350 bps. All trade on paper at the same time, so conditions are identical and the comparison is fair.

02

No free fills

Every shadow entry and exit is priced through the real QuoterV2 at the full 50 USDT size, so the 0.3% pool fee and real price impact sit inside every fill. Gas is deducted per fill at the live gas price. A round trip costs roughly 62 bps before any price move, which is why the tightest band sits at 75.

03

Promotion must be earned

A clerk becomes eligible only with at least 5 closed trades and a positive net P and L after costs. The eligible clerk with the highest net P and L leads; ties break on more closes, then roster order. No eligible clerk means no leader and no live trades. Silence is a valid state.

04

Live, on a leash

Only the leader may trade live, only through ClerkVault, only at 10 USDT a trade. The vault enforces a 25 USDT maximum, a 5 USDT daily loss cap and a one hour cooldown on chain, and the owner can pause it at any time.

Frozen rules. The anchor window, roster, fill model, scoring, promotion rule and vault limits were frozen on 24 November 2026, before the engine collected any data, and are pinned by a SHA 256 hash in the design record. Any change starts a new tournament from zero; nothing is ever tuned on this tournament's own data and presented as its result.
